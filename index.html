Beat the Heat

Survive July in Texas.

I built this itinerary so you're never outside between noon and 5pm without escape. Here are the rules:

  • Mornings outside, midday inside. Cattle drive at 11:30am, then straight to AC for lunch and shopping.
  • Pool is your weapon. Hotel Drover has an outdoor pool, The Joule has the cantilevered rooftop. Use them 1–5pm both Friday and Saturday.
  • Every night thing has AC. Rodeo (indoor coliseum), Billy Bob's, White Elephant, SILO, Cedar Springs bars — all climate-controlled.
  • Pack: hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, big water bottle. Pull a Yeti or Stanley from the hotel mini-fridge before you head out.
  • The cattle drive is short. 10 minutes of walking, 5 minutes of watching. Stand under an awning, snap photos, retreat to AC.
  • Cabs/Ubers everywhere. Don't walk between Stockyards and downtown FW. Don't walk the strip in Dallas — Uber Cedar Springs.

Pro Tips

Things you'll thank me for.

🥵96°F is real

Don't underestimate this. Drink water like it's your job. The midday pool blocks are not optional, they're survival.

👢Boots on Day 1

If you don't own them, hit M.L. Leddy's or Maverick Western Wear Friday morning. You'll wear them all weekend and they make every photo better.

💵Cash for Joe T's

Joe T. Garcia's is cash only. Pull $200 before the trip and you're set for the whole weekend.

👔Elevated Western works

Hotel Drover, Bricks & Horses, The Joule, Knife — all OK with boots + button-down + jeans. No sneakers at the steakhouses.

🚕Uber Saturday night

Park the SUV at The Joule and Uber to SILO + Cedar Springs. Don't drive. Don't even think about it.

🎆Fireworks logistics

Addison Kaboom Town parking is genuinely a nightmare — 500k people. Best play: skip it, watch July 4 fireworks from The Joule rooftop instead before SILO.
