Pro Tips

Things you'll thank me for.

👢Boots on Day 1

If you don't own them, hit M.L. Leddy's or Maverick Western Wear Friday morning. You'll wear them constantly and they make every photo better.

💵Cash for Joe T's

Joe T. Garcia's is cash only. Pull $200 before the trip and you're set for the whole weekend.

🥵It's 95°F by noon

Plan outdoor stuff before 11am or after 6pm. Water like it's your job. Sunscreen at the rooftop pool.

👔Elevated Western works

Hotel Drover, Bricks & Horses, The Joule, Knife — all OK with boots + button-down + jeans. No sneakers at the steakhouses.

🚕Uber Saturday night

Park the SUV at The Joule and Uber to SILO + Cedar Springs. Don't drive. Don't even think about it.

🎟️BRESH sells out

BRESH parties consistently sell out 1–2 weeks ahead. Buy the second you book your flights.
