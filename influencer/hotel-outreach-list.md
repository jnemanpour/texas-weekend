## 0. The 60-second strategy

1. **Fastest wins first.** Four Seasons and Hilton each run an **official influencer request form** (links in §3). Independents and boutique hotels say yes fastest because a local marketing manager can decide on the spot.
2. **Pitch the agency, not just the hotel.** One PR firm (J/PR, Murphy O'Brien, Hawkins/FINN, etc.) represents *many* luxury hotels. One good relationship = multiple properties. See §2.
3. **Right inbox, always.** Marketing / PR / Communications / Social — **never reservations or the front desk.**
4. **Lead with audience quality, not follower count.** Engagement + demographics (high-income, travel-intent) beat raw reach.
5. **Offer usage rights.** The right to reuse your photos/video on their channels is the thing hotels want most — it's your strongest bargaining chip.
6. **Timing.** Pitch **shoulder / off-season** and give **2+ weeks lead**. Peak-season free-stay asks get ignored.
7. **As a couple, sell the multiplier:** two creators, two audiences, a built-in production crew on one reservation.

## 1. Cold-outreach email template

**Subject:** `Content collaboration for [Hotel Name] — [Month]`

```
Hi [First Name],

[One genuine, specific line about the property — the spa, the design, a
renovation, a sister hotel you loved. Proves this isn't a mass blast.]

I'm [Name] — my partner and I create luxury travel content for an audience
of [X] engaged followers (avg. [Y] views/Reel, [Z]% engagement), primarily
[demographic: e.g., 28–45, US, high-income, active travel planners].
[One-line differentiator, e.g., "I'm a software engineer, and our content
over-indexes with a design-conscious, high-spend audience."]

We'll be in [City] from [dates] and would love to feature [Hotel Name]. In
exchange for a hosted stay we'd deliver [e.g., 1 Reel + 1 TikTok + 3–5
Stories, tagged], plus a set of professional photos you're free to reuse on
your own channels (full usage rights included).

Our work + media kit: [link to /influencer]

Would your team be open to a collaboration? Happy to tailor the deliverables
to whatever's most useful for you.

Best,
[Name] | [@handle] | [email]
```

**One polite follow-up 5–7 days later. Don't chase more than twice.** Always disclose (#hosted / #gifted / #ad).
